# Before

- [ ] Confirm the venue, date, schedule, and expected attendance.
- [ ] Assign volunteers to setup, check-in, activities, and cleanup.
- [ ] Prepare supplies, attendee information, and emergency contacts.

# During

- [ ] Brief volunteers on their roles, timing, and safety procedures.
- [ ] Welcome attendees, manage check-in, and share the schedule.
- [ ] Monitor activities, supplies, accessibility, and emerging issues.

# After

- [ ] Confirm attendees have left safely and account for all volunteers.
- [ ] Clean the venue, return equipment, and collect remaining supplies.
- [ ] Record incidents, feedback, expenses, and follow-up actions.
