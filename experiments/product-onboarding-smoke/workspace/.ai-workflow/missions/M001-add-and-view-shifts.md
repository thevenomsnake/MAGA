# Add and view shifts

## Outcome

A volunteer coordinator can add a volunteer name and shift time, then see the current schedule in a phone browser.

## Acceptance

- Adding a volunteer name and shift time makes that shift visible in the current schedule.
- The add-and-view flow remains usable at a phone-sized viewport.
- Schedule data remains local to the browser.

## Boundaries

- No accounts, paid services, publishing, remote storage, or multi-user synchronization.
