# Shift Board

## Product Direction

- Intended user: A volunteer coordinator.
- Problem: Keep a simple current schedule of volunteer names and shift times.
- First value: Add a volunteer name and shift time, then see the current schedule on a phone.
- Delivery: A small local-only browser application inspected on a phone-sized screen.
- Boundaries: No accounts, paid services, publishing, or remote data storage in the first version.

## Current State

The first usable slice is approved and ready to build. Nothing is implemented or blocked yet.
